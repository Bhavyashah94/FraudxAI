## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Foundational Standards & Real-Time Rail Architecture
* Core Definition of Fast Payment (Section 2.1, p. 9):
  * A payment service in which the transmission of the payment message and the availability of ‘final’ funds to the payee occur in real time or near-real time and on as near to a 24-hour and seven-day (24/7) basis as possible.
* Operational Fraud & Security Considerations (p. 6, p. 28):
  * Irrevocability of real-time credit transfers introduces novel operational fraud risks compared to dual-message credit card systems with chargeback recall windows.
  * Rapid automated velocity monitoring and mutual endpoint authentication are critical to mitigate mule accounts and authorized push payment (APP) exploitation.
