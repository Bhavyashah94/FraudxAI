## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Core Payment Metrics (Table B.1, p. 13)
* Aggregate Core Noncash Payments (Calendar Year 2018):
  * Total Volume: 174.2 Billion payments ($97.04 Trillion total value; average ticket: $557).
  * Total Card Payments: 131.2 Billion payments ($7.08 Trillion; average ticket: $54).
  * Debit Cards: 86.4 Billion payments ($3.10 Trillion; average ticket: $36).
    * Non-prepaid debit: 72.7 Billion payments ($2.75 Trillion; average ticket: $38).
    * Prepaid debit: 13.8 Billion payments ($0.35 Trillion; average ticket: $25).
  * Credit Cards: 44.7 Billion payments ($3.98 Trillion; average ticket: $89).
    * General purpose credit: 40.9 Billion payments ($3.64 Trillion; average ticket: $89).
    * Private label credit: 3.8 Billion payments ($0.34 Trillion; average ticket: $89).
  * Automated Clearinghouse (ACH): 28.5 Billion payments ($64.16 Trillion; average ticket: $2,250).
  * Checks Written: 16.0 Billion checks ($26.20 Trillion; average ticket: $1,635).
  * ATM Cash Withdrawals: 5.1 Billion transactions ($0.80 Trillion; average ticket: $156).
* Channel Evolution (p. 2):
  * Remote general-purpose card payments reached $3.29 Trillion in 2018, nearly equal to in-person payments ($3.31 Trillion).
  * Chip (EMV) authentication at physical POS exceeded 50% in 2018 compared to 2.0% in 2015.
