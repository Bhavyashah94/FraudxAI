## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Key Findings (2019-2020 Observations)
* Structural Shift in Consumer Rails:
  * While 2019 showed steady continuation of historical trends, 2020 exhibited a major acceleration toward ACH and remote card transactions (p. 2).
  * Value of ACH transfers grew to 69.25% of all noncash payment value in 2020 (p. 5).
  * Card payments remained the most used instrument by volume, representing over 74% of total noncash transaction counts (Table 1, p. 4).
* Rapid Growth in Remote / E-Commerce and Digital Wallets:
  * In-person card payments experienced steep contractions in Q2 2020 due to physical lockdowns.
  * Remote card payments (e-commerce, digital wallet provisioning, card-not-present bill pay) accelerated by over 20% year-over-year.
