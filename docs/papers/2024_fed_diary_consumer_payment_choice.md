## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Key Findings (October 2023 Survey Cycle)
* Total Monthly Transactions: Consumers made an average of 46 monthly payments in October 2023, an increase of 7 payments compared to 2022 (p. 2, p. 4).
* Payment Instrument Share of Total Payments (Figure 2, p. 5):
  * Credit Cards: 32% (approximately 14.7 payments per month per consumer).
  * Debit Cards: 30% (approximately 13.8 payments per month per consumer).
  * Cash: 16% (approximately 7.4 payments per month; stabilized at a post-pandemic floor).
  * ACH / Electronic: 13% (approximately 6.0 payments per month).
  * Mobile Payment Apps: 6% (approximately 2.8 payments per month).
  * Checks: 3% (approximately 1.4 payments per month).
* In-Person vs Remote / Online Channels (Figure 3, p. 6):
  * In-person transactions: 78% of total purchases and P2P payments.
  * Remote / online transactions: 22% of purchases and P2P payments (up from 19% in 2022, 13% in 2019, and 8% in 2016).
* Cash Holdings & Liquidity Behavior (p. 14):
  * Average daily on-person cash holding: $74 per adult.
  * Store-of-value cash holdings: Elevated across all income strata, averaging $369 to $408.
