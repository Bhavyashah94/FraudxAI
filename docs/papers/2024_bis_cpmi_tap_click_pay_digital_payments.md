## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Key Global Empirical Findings (2022 Red Book Analysis)
* Cashless Payments Velocity per Capita (p. 6, Graph 1.A):
  * Advanced Economies (AEs): Grew from 426 transactions per capita in 2021 to 468 in 2022.
  * Emerging Market & Developing Economies (EMDEs): Grew from 246 transactions per capita in 2021 to 291 in 2022 (+18%).
* Fast Payment Systems as Primary Growth Engine (p. 11, Graph 5.A):
  * Fast payment systems now operate in virtually all CPMI member jurisdictions.
  * Fast payment adoption directly displaces small-denomination banknotes and coins (Graph 6.A, p. 12).
* Travel & Cross-Border Resurgence (p. 13, Graph 7.A):
  * In 2022, cross-border payment card transactions and foreign cash withdrawals expanded sharply as global post-pandemic travel resumed.
