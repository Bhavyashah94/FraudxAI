## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Key Findings & Empirical Benchmarks
* Macro Fraud Loss Totals:
  * By value, total card fraud reached $7.10 Billion in 2015 and $7.07 Billion in 2016 (Table 5, p. 10).
  * Card-not-present (CNP) fraud loss rose from $3.40 Billion in 2015 to $4.57 Billion in 2016 (p. 11).
* Basis Points Fraud Loss Rates (bps = basis points, where 1 bps = 0.01%):
  * Remote / Card-Not-Present Fraud Rate: 18.71 basis points (0.1871% of transaction value) in 2016 (p. 11).
  * In-Person / Card-Present Fraud Rate: 9.34 basis points in 2016, down from 12.17 basis points in 2015 due to EMV microchip rollout (p. 11).
  * Overall General-Purpose Card Fraud Rate: 10.80 basis points across credit and debit rails (p. 10).
  * PIN Authentication Impact: In-person transactions with PIN authentication had a fraud rate of 3.99 bps, compared to 12.78 bps for transactions without PIN (a 3.2x difference) (p. 10).
