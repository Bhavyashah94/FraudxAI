## Executive Summary & Verbatim Research Content

### Verbatim Payment Statistics (Table IX.1: Annual Turnover, April-March, p. 239)
* Unified Payments Interface (UPI):
  * 2021-22 Volume: 4,59,561 lakh transactions (45.96 Billion) | Value: ₹84.2 lakh crore (₹84.2 Trillion)
  * 2022-23 Volume: 8,37,144 lakh transactions (83.71 Billion) | Value: ₹139.1 lakh crore (₹139.1 Trillion)
  * 2023-24 Volume: 13,11,295 lakh transactions (131.13 Billion) | Value: ₹200.0 lakh crore (₹200.0 Trillion)
  * Annual Growth (2022-23 to 2023-24): Volume surged 56.6%, Value expanded 43.8%.
* Credit Cards (Point-of-Sale + E-Commerce):
  * 2021-22 Volume: 22,399 lakh (2.24 Billion) | Value: ₹9.7 lakh crore (₹9.7 Trillion)
  * 2022-23 Volume: 29,145 lakh (2.91 Billion) | Value: ₹14.3 lakh crore (₹14.3 Trillion)
  * 2023-24 Volume: 35,610 lakh (3.56 Billion) | Value: ₹18.3 lakh crore (₹18.3 Trillion)
* Debit Cards (Point-of-Sale + E-Commerce):
  * 2021-22 Volume: 39,384 lakh (3.94 Billion) | Value: ₹7.3 lakh crore (₹7.3 Trillion)
  * 2022-23 Volume: 34,179 lakh (3.42 Billion) | Value: ₹7.2 lakh crore (₹7.2 Trillion)
  * 2023-24 Volume: 22,860 lakh (2.29 Billion) | Value: ₹5.9 lakh crore (₹5.9 Trillion)
  * Migration Trend: Debit card POS/ecom volume declined 33.1% in 2023-24 as consumers switched daily retail purchases to UPI.
* Total Retail Payments:
  * 2023-24 Volume: 16,48,234 lakh transactions (164.82 Billion) | Value: ₹791.5 lakh crore (₹791.5 Trillion).
